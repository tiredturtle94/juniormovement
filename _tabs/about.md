Stress brought me to yoga, and what followed was a transformation in mind and body. Paired with functional strength training and stretching, it gave me a strong sense of self. Now I teach classes that are welcoming and judgment-free, so you can find that same steadiness.

I trained at The Om Lounge Studios in Pittsburgh (RYT-200). 

The name Junior Movement comes from how I like to move: small, grounded, and steady, like a turtle.

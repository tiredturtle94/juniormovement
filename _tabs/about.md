Stress brought me to yoga, and what followed was a transformation in mind and body. Paired with functional strength training and stretching, it gave me a strong sense of self. Now I teach classes that are welcoming and judgment-free, so you can find that same steadiness.

I trained at the Om Lounge Studios in Pittsburgh (RYT-200). 

The name Junior Movement comes from how I like to move: small, grounded, and steady, like a turtle.
## My philosophy

Your body works for you. Even something as simple as holding a plank, the act of holding yourself up, is amazing. One of my favorite sayings is *"Getting older is a privilege. Getting strong is a choice."* I hope to experience both through movement.

In class, I want you to feel challenged but empowered, even after your very first session. The best part is watching the "standard" or "easy" poses, the ones that can still take your breath away, come a little easier and more gracefully over time. When you notice that change, you'll be hooked.

That's how it happened for me. The day I could hold a downward dog for a while, I knew my core had gotten stronger and my posture had improved. I even walk differently now. Those subtle, empowering changes make all the difference, and they're what I encourage my students to look for through consistent practice.
